# Renew your old smartphone into a dumbphone and mp3 player.

There are approximately 7.58 billion active smartphones in the world. However, Analysis by the team at Manochi shows that a total of only 6.12 billion people around the world were using the internet at the start of April 2026, equivalent to only 73.8 percent of the world’s total population. The math shows a clear picture: hyperconsumption at the top paired with a digital divide at the bottom. 


Wealthy consumers hoard multiple devices while billions remain offline, contributing to a huge influx of ewaste and an overexploitation of the Earth for rare earth elements, cobalt, and lithium, as well as miners (even child laborers) being exploited. The demand also causing heavy pollution, child labor, displacement, and arguably, modern day slavery.


The DRC accounts for nearly 70% of global output. While 80% of that comes from large industrial sites, about 20% is still produced via unregulated artisanal mining. 


Tens of thousands of informal workers, including an estimated 40,000 children as young as 7 work in hazardous artisanal cobalt pits without protective gear, earning under $2 a day, and it doesn't end there. 


Massive industrial expansions (such as around Kolwezi and Tenke Fungurume) have also triggered severe pollution, emitting toxic sulfur dioxide levels that breach safety guidelines and harm local communities. Forced evictions and intimidation to clear land for industrial mines are also widespread. 


Ironically, even the recent analog movement where people buy dumbphones or mp3 players to disconnect has been co-opted by corporations into a hyper-consumer trend, resulting in people purchasing additional, trendy secondary devices rather than actually reducing their electronic waste.


## The Solution:
We need to stop looking at purchasable solutions. They are what got us here in the first place. 


What we need is to repurpose and renew our already existing technology.


I'm sure everyone has an old phone, laptop, or computer lying around rotting in a junk pile somewhere. These are Perfectly functioning machines that should be repurposed into a cool & fun hobby project like a custom dumbphone, homeserver, retro time machine, homelab, NAS, cyberdeck, audio player, and so much more.
